\section{Ba{\l}aban's Proposition~1 at block factor~$5$: complete proofs}\label{app:a08_balaban}

This appendix writes out the proof of Ba{\l}aban's perturbative estimate for localised renormalised classes, \cite[Prop.~1, (1.33)]{Ba3}, at block factor $5$, in the form used in \cref{app:a06_BALtorus}. The printed proof on \cite[pp.~420--445]{Ba3} consists of twenty sketched steps and seven further sketched places; each is stated here in the form in which the proposition uses it and proved, with constants independent of the lattice spacing, the step, the domains, the background and the torus. \Cref{app:a06_BALtorus} cites these steps; nothing in this appendix is used elsewhere.
