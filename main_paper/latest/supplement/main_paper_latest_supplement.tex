\author{}
\date{}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
This supplement to the paper on the Osterwalder--Schrader axioms for the three-dimensional abelian Higgs model at fixed photon mass writes out the proof of Ba{\l}aban's perturbative estimate for localised renormalised classes, \cite[Prop.~1, (1.33)]{Ba3}, at block factor $5$. The printed proof on \cite[pp.~420--445]{Ba3} consists of twenty sketched steps and seven further sketched places; each is stated here in the form in which the proposition uses it and proved, with constants independent of the lattice spacing, the step, the domains, the background and the torus. The main paper cites these steps; nothing here is used elsewhere in it.
\end{abstract}
