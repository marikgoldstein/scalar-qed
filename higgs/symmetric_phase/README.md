# The abelian Higgs model in three dimensions: the symmetric phase

**Paper:** [`higgs3d_symmetric_phase.pdf`](higgs3d_symmetric_phase.pdf). The LaTeX source, a single file, is [`higgs3d_symmetric_phase.tex`](higgs3d_symmetric_phase.tex).

The paper studies the Euclidean abelian Higgs model (scalar QED) in three dimensions, regularised on periodic cubic lattice tori. For small couplings it shows that the generating functionals of the curvature and of the renormalised Wick square are bounded on a complex tube, uniformly in the lattice spacing, the torus and the photon mass. The ultraviolet input is Bałaban's renormalisation group, with three repairs of printed proofs.

The limit states satisfy:
- the Osterwalder–Schrader axioms OS0, OS2 and OS3;
- OS1 for the translations and the cubic group;
- clustering on their ergodic components.

Appendix K collects the technical estimates (22 statements) whose proofs are not included in this version. A final section lists the open problems.
