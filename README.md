# scalar-qed

Mathematical documents on three-dimensional scalar quantum electrodynamics (the abelian Higgs model) and its continuum limit. Work in progress.

- [`main_paper/`](main_paper/): Osterwalder–Schrader axioms for three-dimensional scalar quantum electrodynamics at fixed photon mass (versions v0 and latest).

- [`higgs/curvature_rotations/`](higgs/curvature_rotations/): the curvature field of the three-dimensional lattice abelian Higgs model, with full Euclidean invariance and clustering.
- [`higgs/symmetric_phase/`](higgs/symmetric_phase/): the abelian Higgs model in three dimensions, symmetric phase. Torus limits and the
  Osterwalder–Schrader axioms with cubic symmetry.

Each folder holds the LaTeX source and the compiled PDF.
